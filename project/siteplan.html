What are Arepas?

Arepas are round, flat cakes made from ground corn dough or precooked corn flour, which are cooked on a griddle, fried, or baked. They are a traditional food in Colombia and Venezuela, and they can be served with various fillings such as cheese, meats, or vegetables.

Where can you find them?

Arepas can be found in many Latin American countries, but they are especially popular in Colombia and Venezuela. In Colombia, you can find arepas in restaurants, street food vendors, and even in supermarkets. In Venezuela, arepas are a staple food and can be found in almost every household.
